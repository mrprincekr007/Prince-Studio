Before you send.

The fixes people ask about most — short enough to try right now.

Still stuck? Write a message
The APK will not install.

Allow Install unknown apps for your browser, check free storage, then open the downloaded APK again. On some phones the setting lives under Apps → Special access.

My projects are not showing up.

Grant All files access for Prince Studio in Android settings, then refresh the project list. Existing files in the PrinceStudio folder should appear again.

The AI key is missing.

Open Settings, choose your provider, paste the key, save, and try a short prompt. Keys stay encrypted on your device with Android Keystore.

Can I move my projects?

Yes. Export the project as a ZIP, transfer it to the other device, then import it there. The folder stays plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

How quickly will I hear back?

Usually within 1–2 business days. WhatsApp is typically faster for short questions. Detailed bug reports may take a little longer to reproduce.

Do I need an account to get support?

No. You can message the developer directly on WhatsApp. An account is only needed if you choose to publish a project to GitHub Pages.
