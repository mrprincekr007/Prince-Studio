01

1. Scope

Who this covers

This policy covers the Prince Studio Android application (package com.princehacks.princestudio) and this website. By installing or using the app, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not install or use the app.

02

2. Data that stays on your device

Local by default

Prince Studio is local-first. The following data is stored on your phone and is not uploaded unless you explicitly export or publish it, or use a connected feature such as AI that sends the project context you or the agent includes:

  • Projects: every file, folder and asset you create or import.
  • Checkpoints: snapshots used for version restore.
  • Editor settings: theme, tab size, word wrap and other preferences.
  • AI provider keys and GitHub tokens: stored encrypted on-device. They are sent only to the provider or service you configured, never to us.
  • Chat history with the AI copilot: stored locally with the project.
03

Network connections

Prince Studio makes two automatic background connections (configuration check and install counter) and contacts external services when you use a connected feature. Each one is listed below.

04

4. Device and usage information

Install counter

To keep the app working smoothly and to know roughly how many installs are active, Prince Studio generates a random per-install key and stores it in app preferences. It is not your advertising ID, Android ID, name, email or Google account. The key stays on your device across app updates. The app sends it to Firebase at most once every 24 hours when it starts and when you submit support. It is stored with a counted flag and timestamps, and the developer's admin console can read it. It is used only to:

  • show aggregate install counts to the developer;
  • rate-limit the support form and organize support messages;
  • recognize the same installation for those counters across app updates.

Remote configuration is checked periodically while the app is in use; that request does not include the device key. The app does not use advertising IDs, does not include third-party analytics SDKs, and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

06

6. AI features

Your provider, your terms

The AI copilot is powered by the provider you connect in Settings. Prince Studio is a client to that provider. When you use AI features, the app sends your messages, API key, workspace file list, active/entry file contents, project memory, and any files, screenshots or fetched web content the agent reads or captures. That content is handled according to that provider's privacy policy. We do not receive, log or store your prompts or project content on our servers.

Your key goes where you point it.

Prompts, project context, screenshots and fetched pages go directly to the provider you configure. They are not received, logged or stored by Prince Studio. The agent can also fetch public HTTPS pages directly from your device; those sites see your IP address.

We recommend avoiding sending secrets, passwords or private keys in AI chat unless you trust your chosen provider and understand how they handle data.

7. Children's privacy

Under 13

Prince Studio is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

08

8. Security

Encryption and HTTPS

API keys and tokens are encrypted before they are saved on your device. Network connections use HTTPS. However, no method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and credentials safe.

09

9. Data retention and deletion

Delete and uninstall
  • You can delete any project, checkpoint or chat at any time from inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes app-internal data but leaves your project folders on the device unless you delete them yourself.
  • Install markers remain in Firebase until the developer deletes them; there is no automatic expiry. Uninstalling removes the local per-install key but not the remote marker. A reinstall creates a new random key, so the app does not reuse the old marker.
  • Support messages remain in Firebase until resolved and deleted by the developer; there is no automatic periodic deletion.
  • Firebase and Google retain data under their own terms; the developer controls deletion of the app's database nodes.

10. Third-party services

Fonts, links and services

This website is served as static files. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads, and may embed links to third-party sites. The app uses Firebase Realtime Database (Google) to host configuration, install markers and support messages. When you use links to third-party services (for example, GitHub, WhatsApp or your AI provider), their own privacy policies apply. We are not responsible for the practices of external sites.
