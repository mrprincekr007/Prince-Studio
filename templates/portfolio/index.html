3Phases, no drama
  1. 01
    Week 01

    Map the real problem

    Stakeholder conversations, product teardown and a rough point of view. We leave with a clear brief, not a moodboard.

  2. 02
    Week 02—03

    Shape the system

    Flows, interface and a working prototype. We test the risky parts early, while changes are still cheap.

  3. 03
    Week 04+

    Ship the details

    Production-ready screens, motion notes and front-end support. The handoff is a conversation, not a zip file.
