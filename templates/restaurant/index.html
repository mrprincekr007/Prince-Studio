Our story
“The best meals are the ones that make you put your phone down.”
Chef Meera Nair · Tandoor
Since 2024

A little fire.
A lot of feeling.

Tandoor started around a single clay oven and a stubborn idea: that familiar food can still surprise you. We cook with the seasons, source from small farms, and keep the room warm enough to stay a while.

Come for the tasting menu. Stay for the conversation.

Meera Nair Chef & co-founder
